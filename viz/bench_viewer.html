Each cell is one (branch / dsr / method / domain) run. In compare mode pick two sessions: the left column is session A, the middle is session B, and the right shows the per-cell diff — green when B improves on A, red when it regresses. Cells align by their full path. Click an A or B cell for the individual view, the Δ cell for the side-by-side comparison. The cost trajectory charts come from each abstraction's best_history.
